# Privacy Policy — American Odds for Novig

Last updated: 27 August 2026

**This extension collects no data of any kind.**

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal or sensitive user data.
- It makes no network requests. There is no server, no analytics, no telemetry,
  no error reporting and no advertising.
- It reads page content only on `novig.com`, only to find percentages, and only in
  the browser tab in front of you. Nothing read from the page is stored or sent
  anywhere.
- The single preference it saves — whether the extension is on or off — is stored
  with `chrome.storage.sync`, which keeps it in your own Chrome profile and syncs it
  between your own signed-in Chrome installations. The developer cannot see it.
- It does not read browsing history, cookies, credentials, form input, or any site
  other than novig.com.

Removing the extension removes everything it stored.

Questions or concerns: open an issue at
https://github.com/Gamedaytrader/novig-american-odds/issues
